Biography

The Architect of Modern Groove

Pianist, composer, and producer Robert Glasper has created a new musical paradigm. Bridging the acoustic rigor of post-bop modern jazz with the timeless bounce of neo-soul and the visionary poetry of golden-era hip-hop, Glasper’s discography stands as one of the most culturally influential bodies of work in 21st-century music.

From his landmark album Black Radio to scoring Emmy and Peabody-winning motion pictures, Glasper has collaborated with legends including Herbie Hancock, Stevie Wonder, Kendrick Lamar, Erykah Badu, Q-Tip, and Wayne Shorter. His annual autumn residency at New York’s historic Blue Note has grown into an international cultural summit.
