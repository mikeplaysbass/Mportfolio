Biography

New York City's Finest

Bassist, Artist, and Music Director, Michael Decena is professional bass player is based and native in New York City. He is known for a deep pocket, musicality, and adaptability across genres. He has experience in touring, studio sessions, and music direction with various artists like Elmiene, Ricky Martin, Mac Ayres, and Maro.
