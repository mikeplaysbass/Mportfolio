Biography

Made in New York

Bassist, Artist, and Music Director, Michael Decena is a professional bass player that is based and is a native in New York City. He is known for a deep pocket, musicality, and adaptability across genres. He has experience in touring, studio sessions, and music direction across many genres and with various artists such as Elmiene, Ricky Martin, Mac Ayres, and Maro.
